History

The Story of
Chandigarh

1947

Partition & the Vision

After Partition, Punjab's capital Lahore went to Pakistan. India envisioned a brand-new planned city.

🌱
📐
1950

Le Corbusier's Blueprint

Swiss-French architect Le Corbusier is appointed. He designs the grid-sector layout still used today.

1966

Union Territory Status

After Punjab's reorganisation, Chandigarh becomes a Union Territory, serving as capital of both Punjab and Haryana.

🏛️
🌍
2016

UNESCO Recognition

The Capitol Complex receives UNESCO World Heritage status, cementing Chandigarh's global architectural significance.
