Shopper's
Guide

Pro tips for your
retail adventure

🕐

Best Time to Shop

Weekday mornings at markets = no crowds, best prices. Malls are most lively on weekend evenings.

💬

Bargain at Markets

Sector 22 and Grain Market welcome bargaining. Start at 50% of the quoted price and negotiate from there.

🚗

Getting There

Elante and VR Punjab have free parking. For Sector 17, take the CTU bus or auto — parking is tricky.
