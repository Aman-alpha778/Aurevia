International Airport
Gateway to Chandigarh

Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport

Mohali, Punjab (IXC)

The International Airport (IXC) connects Chandigarh to major Indian cities and international destinations including Dubai, Singapore, and London. Located just 30 minutes from Sector 17, it is served by all major carriers including IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, and international airlines. A modern terminal with seamless connectivity.
