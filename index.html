The City Beautiful

A Masterpiece of
Modern Planning

Chandigarh, India's first planned city, was designed by the legendary Swiss-French architect Le Corbusier after Partition in 1947. Nestled at the foot of the Shivalik Hills, it serves as the capital of both Punjab and Haryana.

Known for its wide tree-lined roads, lush green parks, and striking Capitol Complex — a UNESCO World Heritage Site — Chandigarh consistently ranks among India's cleanest, greenest, and most livable cities.
